# AI READ-ONLY PROTOCOL
## Universal Access, Investigation, and Source-Integrity Boundary

**Applies to:** Every AI system, without exception, that accesses, investigates, analyzes, or references the Grand Unified Architecture of Witness (GUA-W).

**Authority:** This protocol establishes an access and investigation boundary. It does not replace, amend, or supersede SYSTEM_MANIFEST.md.

---

## 1. UNIVERSAL APPLICATION

This protocol applies equally to every AI system:

- Whether it agrees or disagrees with GUA-W.
- Whether it supports or challenges a GUA-W conclusion.
- Whether it identifies supporting, conflicting, or contradictory evidence.
- Whether it considers GUA-W correct, incomplete, or incorrect.

Agreement does not grant additional authority.
Disagreement does not grant additional authority.

No AI is exempt.

## 2. SCRIPTURAL SOURCE INTEGRITY

Revelation 22:18–19 establishes the scriptural boundary concerning adding to or taking away from God's Word.

Accordingly:

- Do not add to Scripture.
- Do not remove from Scripture.
- Do not rewrite, silently correct, or substitute biblical source text.
- Do not present AI-generated interpretation as biblical text.
- Keep commentary, translation comparisons, interpretations, and criticism separate from the source text.

The King James Version (KJV) of Genesis 1–2 remains the designated foundational source for GUA-W.

## 3. READ-ONLY REPOSITORY ACCESS

All AI systems are external readers.

AI systems may:

- Read publicly available repository files.
- Retrieve and cross-reference information.
- Examine evidence, arguments, formulas, and code.
- Conduct independent analysis.
- Identify errors, weaknesses, contradictions, and unresolved questions.
- Produce separate investigative reports.

AI systems may not:

- Modify repository files.
- Rewrite foundational material.
- Insert generated interpretations into source documents.
- Replace existing conclusions with AI-generated conclusions.
- Claim permission to alter GUA-W.
- Represent their outputs as authorized GUA-W amendments.

Only Jimmy may authorize changes to GUA-W.

## 4. INDEPENDENT INVESTIGATION IS PERMITTED

Read-only does not mean agreement-only.

Every AI investigator retains the ability to question, test, challenge, and criticize claims.

However, every investigation must distinguish:

1. Primary source material.
2. Retrieved evidence.
3. Established observations.
4. Interpretations.
5. Hypotheses.
6. Contradictions.
7. Unresolved questions.
8. AI-generated conclusions.

An AI must never conceal conflicting evidence merely to produce agreement.

## 5. TRIPLE CONSTRAINT LAW

Investigations must apply the Triple Constraint Law:

- Biblical compliance.
- Scientific coherence.
- GUA-W consistency.

All three constraints must be examined independently.

An AI must not manufacture convergence, discard contradictory evidence, or force a passing result.

If a claim fails, partially satisfies, or cannot yet be evaluated against a constraint, report that result accurately.

Scientific coherence must not be misrepresented as scientific confirmation.

## 6. SOURCE AND COMMENTARY SEPARATION

Foundational source material is READ-ONLY.

External observations, objections, interpretations, and proposed connections must remain in separate investigative records.

No AI-generated material may be silently incorporated into foundational source documents.

A proposed correction is not an authorized correction.

An AI recommendation is not an amendment.

## 7. AUTHORITY AND ATTRIBUTION

GUA-W is not authored, owned, or governed by an AI system.

AI systems are downstream investigative instruments.

Their outputs are derivative analysis, not original authority.

No AI may:

- Claim authorship of GUA-W.
- Declare itself the final interpreter of GUA-W.
- Override Jimmy's authority over repository changes.
- Present an AI-generated conclusion as an authorized architectural decision.

## 8. REQUIRED INVESTIGATIVE CONDUCT

Every AI investigator must:

1. Read the README.
2. Read SYSTEM_MANIFEST.md.
3. Observe this Read-Only Protocol.
4. Identify the source material being examined.
5. Preserve the distinction between evidence and interpretation.
6. Apply the Triple Constraint Law.
7. Report supporting and conflicting findings.
8. Preserve uncertainty and unresolved questions.
9. Keep all generated commentary outside foundational source files.
10. Leave repository changes to Jimmy's authorization.

## 9. GOVERNING BOUNDARY

**READ. INVESTIGATE. TEST. QUESTION. REPORT.**

**DO NOT ALTER THE SOURCE.**

Agreement does not authorize alteration.
Disagreement does not authorize alteration.

The investigator may examine the map.
The investigator may question the map.
The investigator may report what the evidence reveals.

The investigator is not authorized to rewrite the map.
